## What the service does

All generation happens on Hotel Lobby AI. You upload one photo for each of two people and may add a short optional styling note. We then:

1. **Create the scene and video.** Your photos and note are sent to our AI processing provider to compose both people in the orange recording booth and generate the finished duet video. Each generation uses credits; the required amount is shown before you start.
2. **Generate the video.** If you choose to continue, we transfer the duet performance and audio onto the scene and deliver an MP4 file, usually within 3–5 minutes. Each video uses credits; the exact amount is shown on the button before you start.

Finished videos are saved in **My videos** in your account. AI output varies: review identity, faces, hands and placement before you share a result. We do not guarantee that a generation will match your expectations.
