## How to find a Hotel Lobby CapCut template

There are two reliable ways:

1. **From TikTok.** Open a Hotel Lobby video you like. If it was made from a template, there is usually a CapCut "Try this template" link above the caption. Tap it to open the template in CapCut.
2. **Inside CapCut.** Open the app, go to **Templates**, and search for "hotel lobby." Sort by popularity and preview a few — templates with the most uses are usually the most stable.

Then tap **Use template**, pick your photos or clips, and export.

Keep in mind that templates are made by users. They get renamed, removed or replaced as the trend moves on, so the exact template you saw last week may not be there today. Some also need a paid CapCut plan, and availability can vary by region.

No single official one. Templates are made by CapCut users, and new ones appear as the trend spreads.

It may have been removed or renamed, or it may not be available in your region. Search "hotel lobby" in CapCut's Templates tab for current options.

**Can a CapCut template put me and a friend in the orange booth?**
Only if your clips already show that. To generate the scene from two separate photos, use an AI generator.

**Can I edit the AI video in CapCut afterwards?**
Yes. Download the MP4 and import it into CapCut like any other clip.
