## The song: "Hotel Lobby (Unc & Phew)"

Quavo and Takeoff were two of the three members of Migos. In 2022 they worked as a duo, and "Hotel Lobby" was one of the songs they released together. It later appeared on their joint album _Only Built for Infinity Links_.

The duo name explains itself once you know the family tree: Quavo is Takeoff's uncle. "Unc" is Quavo; "Phew" — short for nephew — is Takeoff.

The song is built as a back-and-forth. The two trade verses and lines instead of one leading and the other supporting, and that structure is exactly what made the video so easy to copy.

## The video: the orange COLORS booth

On June 17, 2022, COLORS — a YouTube channel known for single-take performances in front of one solid color — released Quavo and Takeoff performing "Hotel Lobby." The setup was minimal:

- **A flat orange room.** No props, no set dressing.
- **One microphone hanging from the ceiling**, in the middle of the frame.
- **Two performers on opposite sides**, taking turns stepping up to the mic.
- **A steady camera**, so all the energy comes from the two people.

That simplicity is why the image stuck. Anyone who has seen it recognizes the orange wall and the single mic instantly.

## Why the performance means more now

Takeoff was killed in a shooting in Houston on November 1, 2022, a few months after the COLORS video came out. For many fans, the clip became one of the most-watched records of the uncle-and-nephew pair performing side by side. That is part of why people return to it, and why many recreations feel like a tribute as much as a joke.

If you make your own version, keep that in mind: it is a fun format, but it comes from a real moment between two real people.

## How "Hotel Lobby" became an AI trend

In September 2026, creators on TikTok and X started using AI video tools to swap the two rappers for other people — best friends, couples, siblings, celebrities, cartoon characters and even pets — while keeping the original moves, lip-sync and audio.

The format works for AI for the same reason it worked on COLORS:

- **The background is simple.** A flat orange booth is easy for an image model to rebuild.
- **The camera does not move.** Motion-transfer models handle a locked shot far better than cuts and pans.
- **It needs exactly two people.** That makes it a natural "me and my friend" video.
- **The audio is the hook.** Viewers recognize the song in the first second.
