## What is the Hotel Lobby AI trend?

The trend copies a real performance. On June 17, 2022, the COLORS YouTube channel released Quavo and Takeoff performing "Hotel Lobby" against a flat orange backdrop, trading lines on one microphone hanging from the ceiling. In September 2026, creators on TikTok and X started using AI video tools to swap the two rappers for friends, couples, siblings, celebrities and even pets — keeping the original moves, lip-sync and audio.

What makes a clip read as "Hotel Lobby" is a short list of visual anchors:

- **An orange booth** — matte, warm walls with soft light.
- **One hanging microphone** in the exact center, at face level.
- **Two people on opposite sides**, one leading and one reacting, then trading the moment.
- **A locked camera** in a vertical 9:16 frame, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

## What you need before you start

- **Two clear portraits.** One photo per person, face fully visible, no sunglasses or heavy filters. They can be taken separately — the tool places both people in the same scene.
- **Permission from both people.** Only use photos you are allowed to use. For a child, you must be their parent or legal guardian.
- **About five minutes.** The scene arrives in under a minute; the animated video takes about 3–5 minutes.

You do not need CapCut, a video editor, or the original song file. The audio comes with the performance.

## How to do the AI trend Hotel Lobby, step by step

1. **Open the studio.** Go to the [Hotel Lobby AI generator](/#create) and sign in.
2. **Add person A and person B.** Person A goes on the left, person B on the right. Swap the photos if you want the other person to lead.
3. **Pick the framing and length.** "Classic" frames both people from the knees up; "Full body" shows them head to toe. Both are vertical 9:16. Choose an 8-second or 15-second clip.
4. **Add an optional styling note.** Keep it to one detail — "both wearing sunglasses" or "matching black jackets". The booth, mic and positions are already handled.
5. **Press Generate.** Confirm consent, pick a plan if you have no credits yet, and the orange-booth scene with both people appears in under a minute.
6. **Wait for the animation.** The Hotel Lobby performance and audio are transferred onto your two people in about 3–5 minutes. You can close the page — the video is saved to My videos.
7. **Download and post.** You get an MP4 with sound, ready to upload as-is.

If a generation fails, the credits go back to your balance automatically.

## Ideas that work for the trend

- **Best friends:** call and response — one takes the first line, the other answers at the mic.
- **Siblings:** one starts confidently, the other jumps in on the next beat.
- **Couples:** a soft first verse and a shared chorus; let a glance mark the handoff.
- **Parent and child:** keep it playful, and only with your own child.

## Tips for a better first take

- Use portraits with **similar lighting and angle**. A close-up next to a full-body shot is the most common cause of mismatched scale.
- **Neutral expressions** leave room for believable reactions.
- Watch the preview once for **left-right placement** and the **mic in the center** before animating.
- Watch the final clip once with the sound off — the handoff should still make visual sense.
